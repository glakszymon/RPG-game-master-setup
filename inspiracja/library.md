# Library

[Docs](https://mithos.app/docs/) / Library

The PDF Library is a built-in viewer that allows you to organize rulebooks and game documents into a searchable database.

### Adding and Viewing Files

- **Add to Library:** Use the **Add to Library** button located in the left navigation pane to upload your PDF files.
- **Viewing:** Selecting a PDF from the left pane will display the document in the right-hand display area.

### Search and Navigation

- **Search:** Use the **search bar** at the top to quickly find specific terms or rules within your documents.
- **Folders:** You can create folders in the navigation pane and organize your PDFs by dragging them in or out of specific categories.

### Bookmarks

You can create bookmarks for important pages to ensure quick access during a session.

- **Accessing Bookmarks:** Bookmarks are nested directly underneath each PDF entry.
- **Visibility:** Expand the PDF entry in the navigation tree to view and select your saved bookmarks.
