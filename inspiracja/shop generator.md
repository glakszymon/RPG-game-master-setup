# Shop Generator

[Docs](https://mithos.app/docs/) / Shop Generator

The Shop Generator allows you to quickly create varied retail locations, ranging from basic General Stores to specialized Arcane Shops.

### Generating a Shop

To build a custom inventory, configure the following parameters and click the **Generate Shop** button:

- **Shop Type:** Defines the theme of the store (e.g., General Store, Smithy, Arcane Shop).
- **Shop Price:** Adjusts the pricing baseline for the items in stock.
- **Town Economy:** Influences the quality and rarity of the items available based on the local wealth.
- **Assortment:** Determines the variety and quantity of goods in the inventory.

### Inventory Details

Once generated, the tool provides a complete list of items, including:

- **Item Name & Price:** Ready for immediate transaction.
- **Categories & Tags:** For easy sorting of gear, consumables, or magical items.
- **Descriptions:** Flavor text and mechanical details to provide your players during browsing.

### Advanced Shop Settings

The Shop Generator settings menu provides deep customization to ensure every merchant fits perfectly into your world’s economy.

- **Custom Inventories:** Download the sample JSON file to create your own shop types and item lists. Re-import your edited file to expand the generator with your custom content.
- **Currency & Conversion:** \* **Currency Symbol:** Change the currency prefix or suffix to anything from Gold (gp) to Credits (cr) or Dollars ($).
  - **Conversion Rates:** Adjust the exchange rates to scale the economy for high-fantasy or gritty, low-resource settings.
- **Pricing Logic:**
  - **Price Jitter:** Adjust this setting to introduce slight variance in prices each time a shop is generated, reflecting a dynamic market.
  - **Rounding:** Configure rounding settings to snap prices to the nearest decimal, single digit, or tens place for cleaner bookkeeping.
- **Exporting:** Once you are satisfied with a generated inventory, you can save the shop as a **text file** for future reference during your campaign.
