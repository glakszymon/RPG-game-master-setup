# World & Time Tools

[Docs](https://mithos.app/docs/) / World & Time Tools

MITHOS includes three tools for tracking your world state: the **Weather Generator**, the **Calendar**, and the **Time Tracker**.

---

## Weather Generator

Open from the right-click context menu under **World & Time > Weather Generator**.

### Generating Weather

Click **Generate** to produce a weather result. The output typically includes:

- Precipitation (clear, overcast, rain, snow, fog, etc.)
- Wind strength and direction
- Temperature
- “Feels Like” Temerature

---

## Calendar

Open from the right-click context menu under **World & Time > Calendar**.

The Calendar tracks the in-game date. It supports custom fantasy calendars with different month names, lengths, and special dates.

### Setting the Date

Click on the current date display to edit it. Advance the date by clicking the forward arrow or by entering a specific date.

### Custom Calendars

Configure the calendar to match your world’s timekeeping system:

- Number of months
- Days per month
- Month names
- Special events or holidays (shown as markers on the calendar)

If your game uses a standard real-world calendar, the default configuration covers that.

### Tracking Time

Use the Calendar alongside the Time Tracker for combined date and session-time tracking.

---

## Time Tracker

The Time Tracker allows you to manage in-game time precisely, advancing or reversing the clock as your story unfolds.

### Controlling Time

- **Manual Adjustments:** Click the buttons to advance or reverse the clock by **Minutes**, **Hours**, or **Days**.
- **Custom Advancements:** You can create custom buttons (e.g., “+4 Hours”) to quickly jump through long rests or travel periods.

### Visual Indicators

The clock interface provides a visual representation of the day-night cycle:

- **Daylight Hours:** Represented by **Orange** on the clock face.
- **Nighttime Hours:** Represented by **Purple** on the clock face.
- **Customization:** These colors are fully editable in the settings to match your preference.

> **Note:** The duration of day and night is automatically determined by the **Solstice Events** configured in your custom calendar.

### Lunar Tracking

If you have a moon configured in your campaign calendar, the Time Tracker will automatically display the current **Lunar Phase**, helping you track high tides, lycanthropic cycles, or celestial events.
