# Map Display Tool

[Docs](https://mithos.app/docs/) / Map Display Tool

The **Map Display Tool** is designed to cast your battlemaps to an external display at your table.

### Setting Up Your Map

- **Loading Maps:** In the settings menu, click **Battlemap** to select an image or video file to display.
- **Orientation:** You can rotate the map to ensure it fits the aspect ratio and orientation of your external monitor or TV.

### Fog of War (FoW)

By default, the display is covered by Fog of War. You can customize the FoW layer by clicking the **Fog of War button**.

- **Manual Masking:** Use the **Brush** tool to hide or reveal specific areas.
- **Brush Settings:** Use the sliders to adjust the **Brush Size** and **Hardness** for a softer or sharper edge when scrubbing away the fog.
- **Bulk Actions:** You can choose to completely fill or completely clear the Fog of War from the map instantly.

### Grid Options

The tool supports various grid types to match your game system:

- **Grid Types:** Choose between **Square**, **Hex**, or **No Grid**.
- **Cell Brush:** Use the **Cell Brush** to reveal or hide Fog of War by specific grid cells rather than using a free-hand brush.
- **Scaling:** Adjust the **Cell Size** to scale the grid to your map; changing the cell size will automatically resize the tokens on the field to match.
- **Visibility:** You can adjust the **Grid Opacity** to make it as subtle or prominent as you prefer.

### Token Management

- **Visibility:** Use the **Token Options** to toggle the visibility of PC and NPC tokens, as well as their nameplates.
- **Initiative Sync:** Click the **Load Initiative** button to automatically import everyone currently in the **Combat Tracker** directly onto the map as tokens.
- **Token Customization:** Double-click a token on the field to:
  - Add **Colored Rings** (useful for status effects or grouping).
  - **Rotate** the token to indicate facing.
- **Special Tokens:** You can add unique markers or environmental tokens directly from your **Token Library**.
